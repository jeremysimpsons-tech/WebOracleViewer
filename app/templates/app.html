How to connect

Autonomous Database one-way TLS usually uses TCPS on port 1522. Use a wallet when the download includes cwallet.sso.

Unzip the Autonomous wallet on the server. The alias is a name in tnsnames.ora, often ending in _high, _medium, or _low.

Also accepts host:port/service, host:port:SID, tcps://host:port/service, a (DESCRIPTION=...) block, and alias?TNS_ADMIN=/path/to/wallet.
